Community Safety Resources

Practical information to help South African communities recognize hazards and respond safely.

Safety and Preparedness

Road Hazards

Reduce speed near damaged roads and keep a safe following distance.

Report dangerous road conditions through the appropriate municipal channels.

Flooding

Never attempt to drive or walk through moving floodwater.

Move away from flooded low-lying areas and follow official weather warnings.

Electrical Hazards

Stay away from fallen power lines, exposed wiring and damaged electrical cabinets.

Contact the relevant electricity provider or emergency service.

Community Preparedness

Keep emergency contacts accessible and prepare a basic emergency kit.

Share verified safety information rather than unconfirmed rumours.

Emergency Assistance

For immediate emergencies in South Africa, use the appropriate emergency service.

112: Emergency assistance from a mobile phone.

10111: South African Police Service emergency line.

10177: Ambulance and emergency medical assistance.

Availability may vary. Confirm local municipal emergency contacts for your area.
