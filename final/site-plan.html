SafeSignal South Africa

Community Safety & Incident Information

1. Site Name

SafeSignal South Africa

The name combines "Safe" with "Signal" to represent sharing useful safety information and making community members aware of potential hazards. "South Africa" identifies the intended audience and geographic focus.

Proposed domain: safesignal.co.za (availability not verified)

2. Site Purpose

SafeSignal South Africa is an educational community information website that presents safety resources and incident information in an accessible, organized format. Visitors will be able to browse incident records, search and filter information, explore safety guidance, and submit an incident information form.

The project will demonstrate responsive web design, JavaScript, JSON data, asynchronous fetching, browser storage, and accessible user interactions. It is not an official emergency reporting or dispatch service.

3. Target Audience and Scenarios

Target Audience

South African community members looking for general safety information, public incident updates, preparedness guidance, and useful emergency contacts.

Visitor Scenarios

  • Where can I find information about reported road hazards or flooding in my area?
  • How can I filter incident information by category and view more details about a record?
  • Which emergency contact numbers and preparedness resources should I know?
  • How can I submit information about a potential community safety concern?

4. Color Scheme

The site uses a restrained navy, teal, light grey, and white palette to create a clear and consistent information interface.

  • Navy — #123047: Header, footer, primary headings, and strong text.
  • Teal — #287D78: Links, buttons, highlights, and interactive elements.
  • Light grey — #F3F6F8: Page background and content sections.
  • White — #FFFFFF: Cards, text on dark backgrounds, and open content areas.

Text and interactive elements will be checked with a contrast checker. Color will not be the only way to communicate incident categories or status.

5. Typography

Inter — Headings

Inter will be used for headings because its clean letterforms support a modern, readable interface.

Inter — Body text

The same font will be used for paragraphs, navigation, labels, buttons, and forms to maintain consistency and readability across devices.

6. Website Structure

Home — index.html

Introduces the purpose of SafeSignal, highlights selected safety information, and provides navigation to incidents and safety resources.

Incident Directory — incidents.html

Displays at least 15 incident information records generated from JSON data. Visitors can search and filter records and open an accessible dialog for additional details. Each record will contain at least four data properties.

Safety Resources — resources.html

Provides emergency contact information, preparedness advice, hazard prevention guidance, and links to relevant public resources.

Supporting Form Action Page

The incident information form will submit to form-action.html, which will display the submitted form values. This supporting page does not count toward the three-page site requirement.

7. Planned Functionality

  • Responsive navigation with a mobile menu.
  • Fetch JSON data asynchronously with error handling.
  • Generate incident cards dynamically with JavaScript.
  • Search, filter, and sort incident information.
  • Use an accessible modal dialog for record details.
  • Use localStorage to remember a visitor preference.
  • Validate and display submitted form data.
  • Use ES modules and appropriate array methods.
